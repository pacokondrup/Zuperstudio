About the Studio
Zuperstudio
Climate change is the great existential puzzle of our time. Rather than approach climate change
as a problem to be solved
through ever-expanding technological and environmental control, Züperstudio takes the position that
knowing our planet begins with knowing ourselves.
Züperstudio's work looks
outward as well as inward
, exploring the complicated tensions between our otherness
(human in nature)
and our oneness
(human is nature)
.

Through
art, design, and storytelling
, Züperstudio seeks
alternative visions of Earthly symbiosis
.
